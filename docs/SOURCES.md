Source assessment date: **4 November 2026**. Links identify outside sources; their contents are not bundled in this repository.

## Original conjecture and objects

**Luis Ferroni and Daniel McGinnis, *Lattice points in slices of prisms*.**

- [arXiv:2202.11808v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2202.11808v2), dated 23 May 2023.
- [Published article](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/canadian-journal-of-mathematics/article/lattice-points-in-slices-of-prisms/BD4DAAE19B3FBECB840B658A189198ED), *Canadian Journal of Mathematics* 77(3), 2025, pp. 1013–1040; first published online 8 March 2024.
- Definition 3.7 provides the capacity-compatible cycle weights. Conjecture 4.5 is the unit-circle assertion treated here. The source's cycle count is $m+1$; this manuscript calls it $k$.
- Strict positivity of every capacity is essential to the source statement and to the substitution used in this proof.

## Established weighted-Lah identities

**Luis Ferroni, *Hypersimplices are Ehrhart Positive*.**

- [arXiv:1911.10146v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1911.10146v2), dated 12 November 2020.
- Remark 3.11, p. 5: the coefficient recurrence underlying the all-one-capacity recurrence.
- Theorem 3.12, pp. 5–6: the generating function at fixed cycle count. Summing over cycle counts yields the exponential generating function used here.
- Neither identity is claimed as a discovery of this repository.

## Classical root-location input

**Petter Brändén and David G. Wagner, *A converse to the Grace–Walsh–Szegő theorem*.**

- [arXiv:0809.3225v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0809.3225v1), dated 18 September 2008.
- Proposition 1, p. 1, records the classical coincidence theorem for symmetric multiaffine polynomials. It allows open circular regions and requires convexity of the region or full degree.
- We use the convex open unit disk. The theorem is attributed to Grace, Walsh and Szegő; Brändén–Wagner is a precise source for the formulation used, not an attribution of the original theorem to them.
- The manuscript also invokes the classical Gauss–Lucas theorem and proves the needed determinant-coefficient consequence directly. The general stability principle behind that lemma is not presented as new.

The discussion includes Richard Stanley's 5 July 2023 generating-function comment and Ferroni's 6 July 2023 recurrence comment. These are later occurrences of the preceding identities.
